Quarterly Incident Report - Q3

During Q3, the warehouse automation fleet experienced 4 reportable
incidents. Incident INC-1042 involved an ArmFlex-900 unit at the Pune
facility that halted unexpectedly due to a firmware bug in version 4.1.8;
this was resolved by the 4.2.1 firmware update released in August.

Incident INC-1055 was a sensor calibration drift detected at the Mumbai
facility, which was corrected during routine maintenance with no safety
impact. Two minor incidents (INC-1061 and INC-1063) involved operator error
during manual override mode and resulted in updated training materials
being issued to all floor operators.

No incidents in Q3 resulted in injury or significant property damage. The
mean time to resolution across all four incidents was 6.5 hours.
