# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

## Company Overview

Acme Robotics was founded in 2011 and is headquartered in Pune, India. The
company has additional offices in Mumbai and Bengaluru. Acme Robotics
specializes in industrial automation arms and warehouse logistics robots.

## Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working
days in advance for planned leave. Sick leave does not require advance
notice but must be reported to a manager before 10 AM on the day of absence.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are evaluated on a
case-by-case basis. All remote work requires a stable internet connection
and availability during core hours, 10 AM to 4 PM IST.

## Expense Reimbursement

Employees can claim reimbursement for business travel, client meals, and
approved software subscriptions. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of
the expense with an itemized receipt. Reimbursements above INR 50,000
require director approval before submission.

## Equipment Policy

New employees receive a laptop (either a MacBook Pro or a Dell XPS,
employee's choice) and a one-time home office stipend of INR 15,000. Laptops
are refreshed every 3 years.
